# Lutz Attorneys: homepage concept

Concept by Saad Waseem for Lutz Attorneys. **Not the live site.**

A single static page (`index.html`) showing a mobile-first homepage for an
estate planning practice in Toledo, OH. Open it in any browser; no build step.

## What it fixes

The current homepage shows the office address but no phone number, email or
way to book a consultation. This concept puts contact options everywhere:

- **Call** and **Request a consultation** in the hero, the header and a closing band
- A "Reach the office" card next to the hero (phone, email, office)
- A sticky Call / Request bar on phones
- A three-step consultation request: what you need (new plan, review an
  existing plan, or an estate after a death), preferred days and times, then
  contact details, ending in a **Pending attorney approval** status

## Sections

Concept banner, header, hero, services (Wills, Powers of Attorney, Trusts,
Estate Administration), consultation request, about Lonnie R. Lutz, recent
articles, office with map placeholder, footer.

## Placeholders and limits

- Images, the portrait and the map are placeholders. The wordmark is plain text.
- Phone, email, street address and article titles are placeholders for the
  office to supply.
- The consultation flow is a click-through preview. There are no forms,
  logins or payments, and nothing is saved or sent.
